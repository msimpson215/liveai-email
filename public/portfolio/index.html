Axon AI & related ventures

Axon AI is a conversational artificial intelligence that can be built into virtually any website, app, business, product, or digital experience.

It is designed to communicate naturally by voice or text, understand context, answer questions, guide people, take action, perform tasks, and adapt to a specific role or personality within the experience.

Axon is not simply a chatbot or an AI helper added to the corner of a website. It becomes part of the experience itself — an intelligent digital presence that can interact with customers, employees, users, or visitors in real time.

It can be added to an existing legacy website or used as the foundation for an entirely new business, service, application, or product concept.

Its potential applications are broad — spanning websites, apps, products, services, businesses, and connected physical environments.

From that core technology, a growing family of Axon business lines applies the same underlying intelligence to different markets, problems, and opportunities.

Pre-De-Icer Product Family

Pre-De-Icer started with one idea: prevent ice from bonding before a storm instead of fighting it afterward. That original concept has expanded into several related products for different uses.

Pre-De-Icer Live

A liquid anti-icing treatment applied before snow, frost, or freezing rain to help prevent ice from bonding to the surface. Originally developed for windshields, with broader consumer and commercial applications. Extreme-cold performance down to –82°F.

Open Pre-De-Icer →

Traction De-Icer

A granulated version designed to both melt or prevent ice and provide added traction underfoot or under tires. Intended for sidewalks, driveways, parking areas, and other slippery surfaces. Uses calcium / magnesium chemistry for grip as well as ice control.

Commercial / Industrial Pre-De-Icer

A heavier-duty version designed for larger applications such as roads, bridges, ramps, parking lots, fleets, trucks, and other high-risk areas where preventing ice buildup can improve safety and reduce repeated treatment.

Pre-EmberGuard

A temporary protective gel designed to be sprayed on roofs, walls, fences, eaves, and other exterior surfaces before a wildfire ember threat. Apply with a power sprayer or bug sprayer. The coating is intended to help protect the surface during exposure and can later be rinsed away with water.

SiteEye 360 Live

Portable live job-site monitoring + Axon Live

A vehicle- or site-mounted 360° camera and safety beacon for temporary, mobilized work — paving crews, contractors, one-day jobs. The office sees the site live; Axon can talk about what is happening.
