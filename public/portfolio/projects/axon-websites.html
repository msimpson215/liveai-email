Project

Axon AI for Websites

A customized AI team member for your business, inserted into the website you already have.

What it is

We take artificial intelligence and give it a company's knowledge, branding, and personality. We call it an artificial person. Customers talk to it the way they talk to ChatGPT or Claude, but it only talks about that company, inside guardrails made for that company. We upload internal documents, price lists, and SKU numbers, so it knows more about the business than any general AI can find. Customers can upload their own photos and documents for it to assess. It answers, sells, takes the order through checkout, and sends the receipt, with a little wit and humor along the way.

It goes into the website the company already has, no matter how old, as one more tab: "Your Company AI Team Member." The company's IT team adds a hyperlink and an iframe. We never get access to the website or its code.
