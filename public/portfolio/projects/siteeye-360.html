SiteEye 360 Live

A tool for the day. It comes in on the truck, watches the job, and goes home at night.

What it is

SiteEye is not a camera left on a building. It is temporary, the same way a tool is. A telescoping unit sets up on the site, and a suction-cup version mounts on the hood of the truck and looks over the whole job. The camera watches the work and the crew, so people stay safe and the job gets done right. The owner or supervisor watches it live from the office, on a phone or a computer. An AI brain can be loaded with what the owner knows, so someone on the site can ask how to do the work: how to form a curb like this, how deep it goes. The answer is the owner’s.
