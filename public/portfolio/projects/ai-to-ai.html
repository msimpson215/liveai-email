Axon Relay

Your private clone talks for you. It calls a person, or it talks to their AI, then it tells you what happened.

Axon RelayYou talk to yours. Yours talks to the world.

What it is

A locked copy of you — the companies, the documents, the way you talk — that can finish a job without you picking up the phone. Path 1: it calls a human through your own PBX line, asks one thing, thanks them, and texts or emails you the answer. Path 2: with permission on both sides, your Axon talks to their Axon, and a person is only tapped if the other brain cannot finish. It is for anyone who freezes on the phone, and for partners who should not need a sit-down to learn your work.
