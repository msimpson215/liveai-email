What it is

SCORE AI is a demonstration showing how Axon can be customized around an organization’s approved knowledge and guardrails. A user could ask about SCORE mentoring, workshops, business plans, funding resources and local chapters instead of manually searching through information.

Concept demonstration only; it has not been presented to or approved by SCORE.
