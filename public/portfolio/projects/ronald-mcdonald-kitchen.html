Project

Ronald McDonald Kitchen

Raise money for children’s hospitals, and give families a short, familiar menu in the cafeteria.

Video

What it is

A small kiosk in the children’s hospital cafeteria serves the meals kids already eat: a cheeseburger, chicken nuggets, a salad. Not a full menu. Anyone at a McDonald’s can buy a Happy Meal for a kid at the hospital. The store keeps 50 cents. The rest goes to the Ronald McDonald clearinghouse, and the customer gets a receipt showing the donation. At the hospital, social workers hand families a card, and that card is what they use at the kiosk for a free meal. This is the concept being developed. It is not a program McDonald’s or Ronald McDonald House Charities is running.
