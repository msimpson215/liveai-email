Axon AI Patient Education

Scan the QR code on the pamphlet and talk to an AI medical team member about that procedure.

Video

What it is

The pamphlet or notebook a patient gets before a procedure carries a QR code. The phone opens an AI medical team member trained only on that procedure. It is meant to stay inside HIPAA. It answers the basic questions the pamphlet already covers: what the procedure is, how long it takes, and what the preparation involves. A stress test is one example. It does not give medical advice, it does not diagnose, and it does not interpret results. Ask about an allergy and it sends you back to your physician. It is the pamphlet, and it talks.

Any healthcare deployment must use the appropriate privacy, security and compliance safeguards for the information being handled.
