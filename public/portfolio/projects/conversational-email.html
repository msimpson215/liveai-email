What it is

A business sends an email with a subject like “Try our new AI team member.” The blue Axon orb sits in the message. The recipient clicks it, it moves to the side, and it introduces itself: hello, this is A1 Professional Asphalt, I’m the AI team member, and I wanted to tell you about the service. From there it is a real conversation, and it only knows that business.
