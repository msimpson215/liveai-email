Project

No More Boo-Boos

A dissolvable bandage for children, intended as an FDA Class II device. Peel the top off. Warm water does the rest.

Video

What it is

It goes on like a regular bandage, with the same peel-off backing. The film is a light blue. An animal is in the bandage, and warm water is what brings it out. To take it off, peel away the top layer, then hold the rest under warm water and it dissolves. Nothing has to be ripped off the skin. A video of it is coming.
