AI Conversational Business Card

Hand someone your card. They scan it and talk to you — even when you are not there.

Video

What it is

The AI Conversational Business Card puts a QR code on a regular business card. Scan it and an AI that knows you and your business answers questions out loud or by text: what you do, how you can help, and how to reach you. The card keeps working long after the handshake.
