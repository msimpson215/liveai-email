What it is

No more clicking through every brand of orange juice and every loaf of bread. Ask how many kinds they have and what is on special, and it answers. Say put it in the cart. At the end it checks out the whole list: the card is on file, it places the order, and it can have the groceries delivered on the day you name. It also builds a meal. Ask for a recipe for two, gluten free or low salt, and it pulls the ingredients.
