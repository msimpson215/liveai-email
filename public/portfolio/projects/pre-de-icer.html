Pre-De-Icer

Spray it on before the storm. Frost, snow and ice can’t bond to the surface, so they wipe or slide right off.

What it is

Pre-De-Icer goes on before the weather, so ice never gets a grip. It is a family of products, not one bottle: the consumer spray, a road-traction version, a heavier one for commercial trucks, and versions for bridges, roads, driveways, parking lots and walkways, plus boat docks and roofs. Aviation, for runways and aircraft, comes later. How each version is made stays off this page.
