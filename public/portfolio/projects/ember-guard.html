Pre-Ember Guard

An all-natural gel spray for wood and shake shingles that helps keep wind-blown embers from catching.

Video

What it is

Pre-Ember Guard is a formulated, all-natural gel you spray on wood and shake shingles before fire danger arrives. It goes on clear, about as thick as pancake syrup, so you can see the coat build up, to help keep wind-blown embers from catching fire. It can stay up there for days, and it keeps working even after it dries. When the danger has passed, rinse it off with water or let the rain take it off.
