Your stand-in

You talk to yours.
Yours talks to the world.

A private digital twin of you. You give it one task, in plain words. It places the call, or it talks to their AI, then it texts or emails you what happened. You never have to pick up the phone. It does not call unless you told it to.

Two doors. Same brain.

Path 1 · Call a person

Your twin dials Joe.

You say: call this number, ask what it costs to seal 5,000 square feet. It calls through your own Zadarma line. Joe answers as a human. It thanks him, hangs up, and texts or emails you what he said.

Path 2 · AI to AI

Your twin talks to Joe’s twin.

Joe already has a team member trained on his business. You authorize the pair once. Your twin asks his. His twin answers, or it chimes Joe only if a person has to step in. The sit-down does not have to happen.

The twin is you. It is not a public folder.

Résumés, Google Docs, the companies, the way you talk — that lives in a locked Personal Axon, not on the open web. Joe does not get a key to it. He gets a yes/no permission: his twin may ask yours about the work you agreed to share, and you can take that permission back.

1

Knows you

Your companies, your car, your doctor, how you order a pizza. You load it once. You do not re-teach it every time.

2

Does the talking

It handles the whole call, then reports back. You are not still typing replies to the shop.

3

Stays in its lane

One job per call. Ask the price. Order the pizza. Book the slot. Nothing extra, unless you said so. Payment waits until the basics work.

A voice on the line, for people who need one.

Some people freeze on the phone. Some are deaf or hard of hearing. Some cannot speak. The twin does the talking. You read the result, or you type the instruction. Same job as an interpreter, on an ordinary phone call.

You should not have to freeze on the phone to live your life.
