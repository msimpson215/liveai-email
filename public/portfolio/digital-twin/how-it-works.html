The fundamentals

How the twin works, then how it calls.

Three layers. Build them in this order. Layer 1 has to exist before a phone ever rings. Layer 2 is the first useful product. Layer 3 is AI to AI.

Layer 1 — Clone you

Make a private vault, not a public repo

Everything about you stays locked: résumés, Google Docs exports, the companies, how you talk. That is Personal Axon. Putting it on a public GitHub repo would give the world your life. Joe never gets a copy of the vault, and he never gets a key to it.

Load the facts once

Upload the files. Talk it through. “This is A1. This is Pre-De-Icer. This is how I describe SiteEye.” The twin answers like you, inside guardrails you set.

Joe learns from the twin, not from a sit-down

When you want Joe to understand the companies, you grant his Axon a narrow door: it may ask yours about the work you named. You can close that door. He is not “logged into Martin.”

Layer 2 — Your twin calls a person

This is the pizza, the doctor’s phone tree, the battery price, and “call Joe, how much to seal 5,000 square feet.”

You approve one instruction

Out loud or typed: “Call Joe at this number. Ask the cost to seal a 5,000 square foot parking lot. Then tell me.” It does not call unless you told it to. One job. No extras unless you said so.

It places the call on your line

The call goes out through your Zadarma number, so it looks like a real outbound call from you, not a random robot pool. Your twin is the one talking.

It can sit through a phone tree

Press 1. Wait on hold. Stay on the line until a person picks up. A menu is not a reason to hand the phone back to you.

It says who it is

“Hello, I’m Martin Simpson’s AI team member. He asked me to check one thing.” It does not pretend to be you in person. That keeps the other side honest and keeps you out of trouble.

It asks, listens, and stops

It asks the question. If they say “do you want anchovies?” it can answer from your instruction. If they wander, it comes back to the one job. Then: “Thank you. We’ll get back with you.”

It reports to you, not through you

When the call ends, you get a text or an email: what was asked, what they said, anything still open. You did not have to sit on the line.

Payment stays off this layer for now. A credit card on a phone call is a later, locked step, with a one-time limit (“pay only for this pizza”). Do not store cards in the twin. Get one real call working first.

Layer 3 — Your twin talks to their twin

Same instruction from you. No phone. Two brains, both authorized.

Each person has their own Axon

Yours knows you. Joe’s knows asphalt, prices, and your jobs together. Neither vault is shared. His twin can ask yours. It cannot read your files.

You both say yes, once

A pair is a permission, not an API key in Joe’s pocket. “Joe’s Axon may ask Martin’s Axon about sealing quotes.” Either of you can revoke it.

Your twin sends the ask

You still just say what you want. Your twin packages it and sends it to Joe’s Axon. No texting the human unless a human is needed.

Their Axon answers, or taps them

If Joe’s Axon knows the price, it answers. If it does not, it chimes Joe — text, email, or a ping — and waits. Then it sends the result back to yours, and yours tells you.

Layer 2 still matters even after Layer 3 exists. Pizza shops and doctor offices will not all have an Axon. The twin calls a person when there is no other brain to talk to. Same instruction from you either way.

Who it is for

People who freeze on the phone

Ordering a pizza, calling a store, sitting through a hold queue. The twin takes the call. You get the answer.

People who cannot use a phone voice

Deaf people, hard-of-hearing people, and people who cannot speak. They read or type. The twin talks. Same role as an interpreter, without booking one for every errand.

What this is not

Not a public clone

The brain is not a website anyone can open. It is not a GitHub dump of your Google Docs.

Not handing Joe a key

Authorization is a door you open and close. It is not “here is my API key.”

Not you still in the middle

If you are still typing every reply to the shop, it failed. The twin finishes the job, then tells you.

Next build

First, one outbound call through Zadarma: one number, one question, a text or email back. Then load the vault so it can speak as you. Then AI to AI, once two brains exist. Payments, and the fuller accessibility version, come after that.
