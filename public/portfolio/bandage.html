HOW THE FIRST PRODUCT WORKS

Simple enough to understand in seconds.

1

Apply

Peel away the lower protective layer and place the clear bandage over the cut or scrape.

2

Protect + Discover

A small animal character can become part of the experience, giving the child something fun to reveal.

3

Warm-Water Removal

When removal time comes, the dissolvable material is exposed as designed and warm water does the work.

LONGER-TERM PLATFORM POTENTIAL

The children's bandage is only the first application.

Future versions could explore different thicknesses, shapes, mesh structures, controlled-release formats, wound-care uses, burn-care uses, clotting-support concepts, and delivery of compatible topical agents or medications.
