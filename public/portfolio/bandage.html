CONSUMER PRODUCT CONCEPT

Dissolvable Bandage

No More Boo-Boos™

A clear children’s bandage built around the part kids usually hate most: taking the bandage off. Warm water dissolves it — no painful peel.

Open Existing Demo →
HOW THE FIRST PRODUCT WORKS

Simple enough to understand in seconds.

1

Apply

Peel away the lower protective layer and place the clear bandage over the cut or scrape.

2

Protect + Discover

A small animal character can become part of the experience, giving the child something fun to reveal.

3

Warm-Water Removal

When removal time comes, use warm water instead of painfully peeling adhesive.
