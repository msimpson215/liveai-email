THE SIMPLE IDEA

Made for the moment kids usually hate.

The first product is intentionally simple: an everyday children's bandage that goes on normally, but is designed so removal can happen with warm water instead of pulling adhesive from the skin.

1

Apply It

Peel away the lower protective layer and place the clear bandage over the cut or scrape.

2

Make It Fun

A small surprise character — a bear, tiger, or other animal — can be hidden in the bandage so the child gets a playful reveal.

3

Remove With Warm Water

When it is time to remove it, peel the upper layer as designed and expose the dissolvable material to warm water.

LONGER-TERM PLATFORM POTENTIAL

One material concept. Many possible formats.

The children's bandage is the first and simplest application. The broader material concept could potentially be developed into different thicknesses, shapes, mesh structures, timed-release formats, and versions designed to carry compatible topical agents — each requiring its own formulation work, testing, and regulatory pathway.

Different FormsThin films, thicker dressings, mesh formats, and custom shapes.
Controlled ReleaseFuture formulations could explore timed or staged release behavior.
Professional Wound CareLonger-term licensing potential for medical-device and wound-care companies.

The public portfolio intentionally does not disclose proprietary formulation details. Any future therapeutic, drug-delivery, burn-care, surgical, or other medical applications would require appropriate technical testing and regulatory review.
