Dissolvable Bandage

A clear children's bandage concept designed to release or dissolve in warm water instead of being painfully peeled from the skin.

Clear Bandage Concept

Designed to be visually discreet and easier to remove for children.

Warm-Water Removal

The core idea is to avoid the painful peel-off experience by using warm water to release or dissolve the bandage.

Future Potential

The starting point is children's everyday cuts and scrapes, with broader wound-care possibilities to evaluate later.
