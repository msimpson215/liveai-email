FIELD TECHNOLOGY

SiteEye 360 Live

Set the camera up in the morning. Stream live all day. Take it home at night.

A portable live-video system for temporary job sites — paving, sealcoating, roofing, concrete, and other mobilized work. Not permanent surveillance.

1

Arrives with the crew

Portable equipment travels to the temporary job site.

2

Streams while they work

A manager, owner, customer, or office can view the site remotely on phone or laptop.

3

Leaves when the job is done

No permanent camera installation required.

WHAT IT COMBINES

Portable visibility plus an AI layer.

360° Camera

An elevated portable view of the temporary work area.

Remote Viewing

Phone, tablet, or laptop access so someone away from the site can see what is happening.

Axon AI

An AI team member can explain the site, answer questions, and provide context around what the viewer is seeing.

Built around temporary work.
