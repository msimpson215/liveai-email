PHILANTHROPIC CONCEPT

Ronald McDonald Kitchen

A family-support concept built around a simple thought: when a child is seriously ill, the family needs practical comfort too — food, meals, convenience, and moments of normal life.

THE IDEA

Warmth, food, comfort, and participation.

Meals & Kitchen Resources

Practical meal and food support for families spending long periods caring for a seriously ill child.

Small Moments of Joy

Treats, table tents, stickers, and other ways for the community to create a positive moment for a child.

Community Participation

A model where restaurants, donors, hospitals, and local supporters could help families.
