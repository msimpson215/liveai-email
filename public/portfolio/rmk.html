PHILANTHROPIC CONCEPT

Ronald McDonald Kitchen

Two jobs at once: help hospitalized children actually eat — and generate serious revenue for children’s hospitals and Ronald McDonald House Charities across the United States. Corrected money model: about $864K a year in St. Louis metro, scaling to roughly $9.75 million a month / $117 million a year nationwide.

WHERE IT CAME FROM

Born at St. Louis Children’s Hospital.

During his daughter’s treatment, Martin saw that cafeteria food could be excellent — but kids still lit up for something familiar, like a Happy Meal. For months he brought a custom wagon with a cooler and warmers to oncology every Saturday so each child got exactly what they ordered. The goal was simple: make sure the children ate, and felt remembered.

Ronald McDonald Kitchen turns that personal tradition into a scalable program: comfort for kids, and real revenue for the hospitals and houses that care for them.

THE DOUBLE PURPOSE

Feed the kids. Fund the hospitals.

Keep children eating

Familiar meals — Happy Meals, cones, nuggets — delivered through hospital kitchen / kiosk access so kids in treatment get comfort food they will actually eat.

Raise money for children’s hospitals

Customers donate at the register. After a small franchise cut, the balance goes to a Ronald McDonald House bank account. Part runs the hospital kiosk; surplus funds children’s hospitals and Ronald McDonald House — a national revenue engine, not a one-off ask.

Self-sustaining model

On the corrected money sheet: restaurants keep $0.50 per donated Happy Meal as passive income; about $2.50 of each $2.99 meal goes to the RMH account. Kids get meals. Hospitals get growth capital.

HOW IT WORKS

Donate at McDonald’s. Redeem in the hospital.

1

Customer donates

At checkout or the app: “Would you like to donate a Happy Meal or cone to a child at Children’s Hospital today?” Donor gets a sticker: “I Made a Child Smile Today.”

2

Money splits cleanly

Example from the corrected money sheet: 10 Happy Meals @ $2.99 = $29.99 at one restaurant. $5.00 ($0.50 × 10) stays with that restaurant; $24.99 goes to the Ronald McDonald House bank account.

3

Child redeems in hospital

Social workers issue meal cards. Kids redeem at a Ronald McDonald Kitchen kiosk inside the hospital cafeteria. A portion of the $24.99 operates the kiosk; excess funds the hospital and Ronald McDonald House.

LONG-TERM VISION

Start in St. Louis. Grow nationwide.

Launch first at St. Louis Children’s Hospital — where the idea was born — then expand to children’s hospitals across the country. If one retail partner does not adopt it, the same donation-to-hospital-kitchen model can adapt to other family-friendly chains.
