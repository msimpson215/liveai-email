Technology and business applications

Axon AI for WebsitesCustomers talk to that company’s own AI on the website it already has. Axon AI OperatorYou speak. It opens email, finds documents, and moves the work on the computer. Talking Business CardSomeone scans the card and can ask what you do, even after you have left. Conversational RésuméA hiring manager talks to the résumé and hears only that one person’s background. Conversational EmailThe email carries an AI the reader can talk with about that one company. Axon AI PointPeople keep the presentation and can still ask it questions after the meeting. Personal AxonOne private AI for one person. Only that person can open it. Axon Digital TwinYou give it one task. It makes the call, or talks to their AI, then reports back. SiteEye 360 LiveA job-site camera that rides in on the truck, lets the office watch live, and goes home at night. Axon AI Patient EducationA patient scans the pamphlet and asks basic questions about that procedure. It does not diagnose. Axon AI Product InstructionsScan the code on the box. The AI walks you through putting the product together or using it. Grocery example · DierbergsGrocery ShoppingOne example of Axon helping a shopper. At Dierbergs it talks through the choices, fills the cart, and checks out.

Inventions and products

Pre De-IcerSpray it on before the storm. Ice does not bond to the surface, so it wipes or slides off. Pre Ember GuardA clear gel sprayed on wood and shake shingles before fire weather, so wind-blown embers are less likely to catch. No More Boo-BoosIt goes on like a regular bandage. Warm water dissolves it, so it does not have to be ripped off the skin. An animal shows in the bandage.
