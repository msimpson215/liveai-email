PREVENT BEFORE IT BONDS

Pre-De-Icer

A preventive anti-icing concept that began with a simple problem: instead of waiting for frost, snow, or freezing rain to bond to a windshield, treat the surface before the storm.

PRODUCT FAMILY

One Core Idea. Multiple Applications.

The original windshield concept has expanded into a family of products aimed at different surfaces, users, and operating environments.

Pre-De-Icer

Liquid anti-icing treatment applied before frost, snow, or freezing rain to help prevent ice from bonding to the surface. The original focus is the windshield.

Traction De-Icer

A granulated version designed to address ice while also adding traction underfoot or under tires for sidewalks, driveways, parking areas, and similar surfaces.

Commercial / Industrial

A heavier-duty direction for roads, bridges, ramps, parking areas, fleets, trucks, and other larger-scale or high-risk applications.

Pre-EmberGuard

A related temporary protective-gel concept for exterior surfaces such as roofs, walls, eaves, and fences before a wildfire ember threat.

Future Development — Aviation

A future research direction exploring anti-icing applications for aircraft and runways.
