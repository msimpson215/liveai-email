PRODUCT FAMILY

One preventive idea, several directions.

CONSUMER

Windshield Pre-De-Icer

The primary marketing focus: a spray-on product used before winter weather to reduce ice bonding on vehicle glass.

HOME

Walkways & Driveways

Household use for steps, sidewalks, driveways, and other surfaces where preventive treatment may be useful.

TRACTION

Traction De-Icer

A granulated concept intended to combine ice control with added traction. Exact formulation details remain proprietary and subject to final development.

COMMERCIAL

Commercial / Industrial

Heavier-duty or higher-viscosity versions for fleets, trucks, bridges, ramps, parking areas, and other large-scale applications.

MARINE / SPECIALTY

Specialized Formulations

Future versions could be adjusted in viscosity or delivery for specialized environments where a standard consumer spray is not appropriate.

FUTURE

Aviation Development

A future research direction for aircraft and runway anti-icing applications.

RELATED CONCEPT

Pre-EmberGuard

▲ ▲ ▲

Inspired by destructive wildfire and ember events, Pre-EmberGuard is a concept for a temporary exterior protective coating that could be applied to roofs, eaves, walls, fences, and other vulnerable surfaces before an ember threat.

The intended concept is a nonflammable temporary coating that can later be washed away with water, including after drying. Final performance claims would depend on formulation testing and validation.
