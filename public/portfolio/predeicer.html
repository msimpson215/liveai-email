PREVENT BEFORE IT BONDS

Pre-De-Icer

A liquid anti-icing treatment applied before snow, frost, or freezing rain to help prevent ice from bonding to the surface. Originally developed for windshields — apply before the storm.

PRODUCT FAMILY

One preventive idea, several directions.

CONSUMER

Windshield Pre-De-Icer

Primary marketing focus: spray on before winter weather so ice is less able to bond to vehicle glass. Extreme-cold performance down to –82°F.

HOME

Walkways & Driveways

Household use for steps, sidewalks, driveways, and other exposed surfaces.

TRACTION

Traction De-Icer

A granulated version designed to melt or prevent ice and provide added traction underfoot or under tires — for sidewalks, driveways, and parking areas. Calcium / magnesium chemistry for grip as well as ice control.

COMMERCIAL

Commercial / Industrial

Heavier-duty version for roads, bridges, ramps, parking lots, fleets, trucks, and other high-risk areas where preventing ice buildup improves safety and cuts repeated treatment.

WILDFIRE

Pre-EmberGuard

Temporary protective gel sprayed on roofs, walls, fences, and eaves before a wildfire ember threat. Apply with a power sprayer or bug sprayer; rinse away with water afterward. Concept inspired by destructive California wildfire / ember events.

FUTURE

Aviation Development

Future research direction for aircraft and runway anti-icing. Clearly labeled future development — not an approved aviation product.
