Specialized Testing Services

STS pioneered a national STAT STD-testing model in 1993: a call center, major laboratory networks, and existing Patient Service Centers instead of a chain of owned clinics. Same-day appointments, next-day results. Annual gross sales about $1.4 million.

The idea was simple and hard: use infrastructure that already existed, staff who knew the medicine, and a phone that actually got answered.

Medical testing center

From that lab-and-PSC pattern he built medical testing companies, including Medical Testing Solutions — nationwide DNA paternity testing run as a call center out of the St. Louis metro, using LabCorp and other collection sites. Marketing was in-house geo-SEO rather than expensive pay-per-click, so the test could be priced below typical internet competitors.

Annual gross sales on this medical testing work were about $4 million — not the smaller figure that sometimes shows up on old drafts.

Restaurants

He owned and operated restaurants, including My Cafe, Momo’s, and Boogaloo’s. That operating history also informs the later fundraising proposal for children’s hospitals: a restaurant-based giving model, not a speech.
