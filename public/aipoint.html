The problem

Presentations only go one way.

  • Questions wait until the end, or never get asked.
  • The presenter can't be in every room.
  • A deck sent by email can't explain itself.

How it works

Click through the slides. Tap the orb to ask.

  • Each page is a slide, on a phone or a laptop.
  • The assistant knows which slide is showing and can explain it.
  • It answers by voice, from the company's approved information only.

Where it fits

Anywhere a deck has to do the talking.

  • Sales pitches and investor decks
  • Employee training and onboarding
  • Client briefs sent as one link by text or email
