How it works

Set it in seconds. Watch from anywhere.

Two ways to mount, one live feed. No IT department, no wiring, no checklist.

1

Mount it

Twist-lock suction base grips the truck hood — turn to lock, turn back to release, no paint damage. Or drop the telescoping pole on a weighted jobsite tripod for indoor remodels.

2

It connects itself

Built-in 5G hotspot (AT&T, T-Mobile, or Verizon) links up automatically. Wherever there's signal, you're live.

3

Watch live

Open the dashboard on your monitors or the app on your phone. Click any site — like a Ring camera for your whole operation.

4

Pack it up

End of day, the pole collapses into the truck. Feeds auto-expire in 24 hours — keep a clip when you need the proof.
