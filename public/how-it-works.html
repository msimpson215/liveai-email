How It Works

Three steps. No wiring. No training manual. Just mount it and go.

  1. Install the mount Attach the SideEye360 bracket to the front passenger-side corner of the truck — fender, bumper, or body panel. One-time setup per vehicle.
  2. Seat the unit Slide the SideEye360 device into the mount before the driver pulls out. The open-circuit connection completes on contact, and the red LED starts flashing automatically.
  3. Visual warning is live Anyone approaching from the passenger side sees a flashing red alert. When the unit is removed from the mount, the circuit opens and the LED shuts off — saving battery until next use.

Why open-circuit?

Most safety devices fail because someone forgot to turn them on. SideEye360 flips that problem: the mount is the switch. Put it in the bracket and it's on. Take it out and it's off. No extra step for your crew to remember.
