How It Works

Installs in under 30 seconds. No tools. No training manual.

  1. Arrive at job site Park and set the brake. Hood mount is already on the truck from one-time setup.
  2. Insert pole Drop the telescoping pole into the hood mount and secure the locking pin. Power, beacon, and camera activate automatically.
  3. Auto connect The in-cab hotspot phone links up on its own. Live 360° stream is ready — no switches to flip.
  4. Watch live View the full 360° job site from your office on any device. Improve safety, ensure quality, watch from anywhere.
  5. Collapse & go Remove the pole, collapse it, and stow in the truck. Open circuit shuts everything off until next job.
