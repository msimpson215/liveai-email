System Specs

Hardware details for fleet managers and installers. Final production specs may be updated at launch.

LED

High-output red LED, flashing pattern optimized for daylight visibility

Power

Battery operated — replaceable cells, no vehicle wiring required

Activation

Open-circuit auto-on when seated in mount; off when removed

Mount

Passenger-side front corner bracket — bolt-on, weather-resistant

Environment

Built for outdoor fleet use — vibration, rain, and temperature swings

Install

Mount once per vehicle; unit swaps in and out in seconds

Compatibility

Designed for commercial trucks, tow trucks, utility vehicles, and work fleets with a front passenger-side mounting surface. Contact us for fitment questions on specific makes and models.
