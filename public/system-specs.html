Mount specifications

Bumper clamp

Steel clamp, rubber pads, hand-tighten knob. Fits most pickup and commercial front bumpers. No drill.

Suction twist-lock

6"+ industrial cup, lever-actuated vacuum. Rated for highway vibration when locked. Twist off — no residue.

Tripod stand

Weighted base, 3-leg spread, quick-release pole collar. Use on pavement, gravel, or compacted job pads.

Hood pin (optional)

Red safety base, reflective stripes, locking pin. Requires drill-once install. For dedicated fleet trucks only.
