How It Works

From pamphlet to conversation

Example: a nuclear stress test.

The patient gets the pamphlet

At scheduling or at the front desk.

They scan the QR code

The AI guide opens on their phone. No app needed.

They ask out loud

“Can I drink coffee before?” “How long will I be there?”

Anything clinical goes to the care team

It points them to their doctor for advice or results.

This guide is for general education only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or interpretation of results. Always consult your cardiologist or primary care provider.
