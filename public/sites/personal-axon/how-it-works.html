How it works

Personal Axon is built around a single life. The job, the company, relationships, money, what they follow, what they care about. Martin Simpson’s holds Martin Simpson, and nobody else. It is locked to that person, by fingerprint or iris. They can pull up a résumé, talk through a decision, or use it as an advice tool. It is customized with everything about them, and it is theirs.
