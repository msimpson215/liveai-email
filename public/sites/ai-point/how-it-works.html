How it works

Each person in the room gets a link to the presentation and follows along on a phone, tablet, or laptop. The presenter has it programmed for that project only. When the meeting is over they still have it. They can keep asking questions, get answers, and reach the presenter by email or phone. It is their own team member, attached to the deck, that they take with them.
