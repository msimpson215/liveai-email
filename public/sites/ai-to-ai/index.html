Two doors. Same brain.

Path 1 · Call a person

Your Axon dials Joe.

You say: call this number, ask what it costs to seal 5,000 square feet. It calls through your own phone line. Joe answers as a human. It thanks him, hangs up, and texts or emails you what he said.

Path 2 · AI to AI

Your Axon talks to Joe’s Axon.

Joe already has a team member trained on his business. You authorize the pair once. Your Axon asks his Axon. His Axon answers, or it chimes Joe only if a person has to step in.

The clone is you. It is not a public folder.

Résumés, Google Docs, the 15 companies, the way you talk — that lives in a locked Personal Axon, not on the open web. Joe does not get a key to it. He gets a yes/no permission: his Axon may ask yours about the work you agreed to share, and you can take that permission back.

1

Knows you

2

Does the talking

It is not a text relay you still have to run. It handles the whole call, then reports back.

3

Stays in its lane

One job per call. Ask the price. Order the pizza. Book the slot. Nothing extra, unless you said so.

You should not have to freeze on the phone to live your life.
