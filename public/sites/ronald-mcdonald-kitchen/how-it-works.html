How It Works

From one meal to many

A kiosk in the cafeteria

Placed in children’s hospitals.

Families get meal cards

Handed out by hospital social workers.

The public buys a meal

At any participating restaurant.

Money goes to the families

50¢ to the restaurant, the rest to the charity, and the buyer gets a receipt.
