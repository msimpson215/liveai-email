How It Works

On easy. Off easier.

Put it on

Just like a regular bandage.

Peel the top layer

The animal comes off first.

Add warm water

The rest dissolves away.

No More Boo-Boos is in development and is intended to be submitted as an FDA Class II medical device. It is not yet cleared for sale.
