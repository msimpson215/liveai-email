How it works

Instead of one more piece of paper, the applicant loads their background and the answers to the questions interviewers actually ask, anywhere from a hundred common ones up to a thousand. The employer talks to it the way they talk to any AI, and every answer stays on that person’s résumé. It does not talk about anyone else. Guardrails are part of it: it will not take a question it should not answer, such as whether the person has a criminal record. A separate company comes next, on the employer’s side. That AI already knows the company, its job descriptions and its numbers, and it reads a whole stack of résumés through the owner’s eyes.
