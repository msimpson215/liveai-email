How it works

Older websites that ranked well for years are showing up less. People now ask an AI instead of clicking through search results, and the businesses behind those sites lose the revenue. Axon puts a team member inside the site a company already owns. It is not a chatbot and it is not a canned assistant. It is the same kind of conversational AI people use every day, trained only on that business. It can answer questions, describe the service, and sell. On a catalog site it can know every SKU. It can take an order through checkout, send the receipt, and take payment. It does whatever that business is programmed to do.
