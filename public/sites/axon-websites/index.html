What your team member can do

1

Hold a real conversation

Customers talk to it the way they talk to ChatGPT or Claude. The difference is that it only talks about your company.

3

Customers can upload photos and documents, and it assesses them for your industry.

4

Sell

It answers product questions, finds the right SKU, takes the order through checkout, and sends the receipt.

Stay inside your guardrails

We set the rules for your company, so it stays on topic and on message.

6

Have a personality

Warm, quick, a little wit and humor, and always sales supportive. We tune it to sound like you.
