Historic Maplewood

In the St. Louis area he developed historic Maplewood property — century-old buildings on the Sutton Loop and Manchester Road corridor, collected and rehabbed with an eye toward the neighborhood’s history. That work sold as a portfolio for about $3.4 million.

He described it as collecting buildings the way some people collect rare coins: structures with a story, that needed attention and a new vision. He wanted to do for the Sutton Loop what Joe Edwards did for the University City Loop — even talked about bringing back a streetcar.

Florida and Michigan

Development did not stop at the metro. He has done out-of-state property work in Florida — including Cape San Blas on the Gulf — and Michigan.

Rental houses

Alongside larger development, he has owned and operated rental houses — the unglamorous side of property: tenants, upkeep, and the long hold.
