Where He Comes From

Upbringing & Education

Born at Stanford Hospital

Palo Alto, California

Martin was born at Stanford Hospital and raised in Palo Alto, where he went to grade school, middle school, and Palo Alto High School. He grew up there before and during the creation of Silicon Valley.

What shaped him most was the community itself: open-minded, intellectual, and right next to Stanford. The friends he still keeps in touch with from those years all recognize what a beautiful place it was to grow up.

Class of 1987

Washington University in St. Louis

Martin came to St. Louis from California to attend Washington University on a full tuition scholarship. He earned degrees in biology and sociology and graduated in 1987.

He was headed toward neuroscience. That is where the name Axon comes from.

Career Background

Before building Axon AI and SiteEye 360 Live, Martin spent three decades founding operating companies, organizing national laboratory testing networks, developing commercial real estate, running restaurants, and inventing patented products.

His career began in public health during the HIV/AIDS crisis at Doorways and the Missouri Department of Health, where seeing two-week wait times for test results led him to organize the first coast-to-coast rapid HIV/STI laboratory network in 1993 — followed by nationwide DNA paternity testing, the historic Maplewood family entertainment district, and children’s hospital philanthropy.

Career Chronology

  1. Palo Alto, California — Born at Stanford Hospital; grade school, middle school, and Palo Alto High School.
  2. Education & Public Health — Washington University in St. Louis (1987, full scholarship); Doorways, Missouri Department of Health, and HIV crisis trainer of trainers.
  3. Specialized Testing Services — Pioneered the first national lab-network model for rapid HIV/STI testing (~1993).
  4. Nationwide Medical Testing — Diagnostic call centers, regional laboratory partnerships, and DNA paternity testing nationwide.
  5. Real Estate & Hospitality — Historic Maplewood district, Florida beachfront subdivisions, Michigan properties, and Maya Cafe, Momo’s, and Boogaloo’s.
  6. Patents & Product Inventions — Pre-De-Icer™ (U.S. Patent #8,119,025), No More Boo-Boos dissolvable bandage, Pre-Ember Guard, and Ronald McDonald Kitchen hospital concept.
  7. SiteEye 360 Live — Portable live job-site camera monitoring paired with owner-trained AI field guidance.
  8. Axon AI — Custom-trained artificial brains and conversational AI team members for businesses, websites, email, and voice.

Current Focus

Axon AI and SiteEye 360 Live — building practical AI brains and field systems for businesses, alongside continued diagnostic and product ventures.
