Overview

Martin David Simpson

This is an Axon AI Interactive Resume — not a PDF to skim and file away. Tap the orb, ask a question, and have a conversation about Marty’s career.

Martin David Simpson started in public health during the HIV/AIDS crisis — Doorways, the Missouri Department of Health, CDC training — then left to build what did not exist yet: a national network of patient service centers so someone could call one number and get an HIV test anywhere in the country, with results back in days instead of weeks. Roughly six regional laboratory relationships, coast to coast, before Quest or LabCorp consolidated the industry. He is proud of that one.

The same philosophy runs through everything after it: counseling and risk assessment first, then a system that actually gets a person tested, housed, or answered. In 1994 he invested in a legal fight that lasted a year and a half. This file does not invent the case name.

From there: medical testing companies at national scale, historic property development, restaurant ownership, patents, and the voice-to-voice AI work this site is built on.
