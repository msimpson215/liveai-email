Career

Businesses & Ventures Founded

After Washington University, Martin went into public health during the HIV/AIDS crisis, at Doorways and the Missouri Department of Health. People were waiting two weeks for their test results. So in 1993 he founded Specialized Testing Services and organized the first coast-to-coast rapid HIV/STI laboratory network.
