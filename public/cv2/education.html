Public health

The professional education that matters on this CV starts in public health during the HIV/AIDS crisis — including work connected to the Missouri Department of Health and CDC training.

From that foundation he fought for, and succeeded in creating, a national network of patients using multiple regional labs in the United States. He advocated for virtual medicine. In 1994 he invested in a legal fight that lasted a year and a half. Ask him for the case name if you need it; this page will not invent one.

That is the foundation under the testing companies: how systems fail people, how a phone line and a protocol either work or they do not, and why “we’ll get back to you” is not an answer when someone is scared.

What the companies taught

The rest of the education is operating: call centers, labs, restaurants, historic property, patents, and now voice AI. This page does not invent degrees or certificates that are not in the file. If you need a conventional transcript line, ask him.
