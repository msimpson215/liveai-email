Pre-De-Icer

Preventive ice treatment invented by Martin Simpson. You apply it before the storm. A thick, honey-like film clings so ice does not bond. Wipers, not scrapers. Salt-free and eco-safe. Available for sale or licensing.

No More Boo-Boos (Dissolvable Bandage)

A bandage designed so nothing has to be ripped off fragile skin — aimed first at children, with broader applications in elderly care, dialysis, and burns. Peel away the top layer, hold the remaining film under warm water, and it dissolves cleanly. FDA clearance has not been granted.
