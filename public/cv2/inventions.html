Pre-De-Icer™ — U.S. Utility Patent #8,119,025

Patented preventive ice treatment invented by Martin Simpson. You apply it before the storm. A thick, honey-like film clings so ice does not bond. Wipers, not scrapers. Salt-free and eco-safe. Patented, not yet commercialized; available for sale or licensing.

That is the “U.S. Patent Holder” line on the home page. Anti-icing is the issued patent.

Dissolvable bandage — provisional

A bandage that dissolves under warm water so it does not tear skin — first for children, with a longer path into fragile skin, dialysis, burns, and licensing rather than a fight with the names already on the drugstore wall. Working names have included No More Boo-Boos and No More Ouchies. The next legal step is the provisional. This page does not claim FDA clearance it does not have.
