COMMON QUESTIONS

Start with the basics

What is the difference between a legal and home paternity test?

A legal test uses documented identity verification and chain of custody so the result can be used for appropriate official purposes. A home test is for personal knowledge and does not use that legal collection process.

Can people test in different states?

For many legal cases, participants can be collected at different approved locations. The exact arrangement is confirmed when the case is set up.

Does prenatal testing require a sample from the baby?

No sample is taken directly from the baby. The process uses a maternal blood sample together with a DNA sample from the alleged father.

What if I start with AI and decide I want a person?

Use the human callback option at any time. AI is simply another way to begin the process.
