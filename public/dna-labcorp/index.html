How It Works

A simple process built around choosing the right test and arranging the proper collection method.

1. Choose your testLegal, home, or prenatal testing depending on how you intend to use the result.
2. Arrange collectionThe appropriate collection process is coordinated for the testing option selected.
3. Receive your resultLaboratory testing is completed and the result is provided through the appropriate secure process.

Legal DNA Paternity Testing

Legal paternity testing uses documented identity verification and a chain-of-custody collection process so the laboratory result can be used for appropriate legal or official purposes.

Home DNA Testing

Home paternity testing is intended for personal knowledge and peace of mind. It uses a convenient private collection process without the formal chain-of-custody requirements of a legal test.

Non-Invasive Prenatal Paternity Testing

Prenatal paternity testing can establish paternity before birth using a maternal blood sample together with a DNA sample from the alleged father. Final availability and collection requirements are confirmed before testing.
