Put orb in Gmail

Step 1: Download orb.gif

  2. Bottom of compose window → click Insert photo (mountain icon)
  3. Click Upload tab (not Web URL)
  4. Pick orb.gif from Downloads or Desktop
  5. The yellow orb appears in your email
  6. Click the orb → click Link (chain icon) → paste this:
    https://liveai-email.onrender.com/launch.html?src=email
  7. Send to yourself → open email → click orb
