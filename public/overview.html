Overview

A simple, always-visible safety light for the front passenger side of your truck — so pedestrians, cyclists, and other drivers see you before it's too late.

What is SideEye360?

SideEye360 is a battery-operated LED warning device that mounts on the front passenger-side corner of commercial trucks and work vehicles. When installed in its mount, an open-circuit connection automatically powers a flashing red LED — giving everyone around the vehicle a clear visual heads-up that the truck is active.

Built for safety, not surveillance

This isn't about tracking employees or watching drivers. SideEye360 is a passive visual warning system — the same idea as a backup light or hazard indicator, but positioned where people actually look when a truck is pulling out, turning, or working roadside.

Why the passenger side?

The front passenger corner is the blind spot drivers worry about most — especially in wide commercial trucks. A flashing red LED at eye level on that corner gives pedestrians and oncoming traffic an unmistakable signal that the vehicle is in operation.
