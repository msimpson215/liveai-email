Questions about your stress test?

Scan this and just talk. No app, no login, no typing. Ask as many times as you want, today or next week.

  1. Point your camera at the code
  2. Tap the circle
  3. Ask anything about the test

An AI guide, not a person. It does not discuss your results, your symptoms, or your medications — your care team handles those.

Open the line Print sheet of 8 Waiting-room poster Concept sheet
What it will and won't do. It explains what the test is, what happens step by step, the medication version, the caffeine rule, how to get ready, how long it takes, and when results come back. It refuses to interpret results, weigh in on symptoms, or say anything about medications — every time, it sends those to the physician. If someone describes chest pain or trouble breathing happening right then, it stops and tells them to get a nurse immediately.

Prototype. The medical wording needs a clinician's sign-off before this goes in front of a real patient.
