Features

Designed so it actually gets used — no switches to forget, no wiring to install, no complicated setup.

  • Flashing red LED High-visibility alert light that catches attention in daylight and low light. Pedestrians and drivers see it from the passenger-side approach angle.
  • Auto-activate in mount Open-circuit design means the LED turns on the moment the unit seats in its truck mount. No power switch — employees can't forget to turn it on.
  • Battery operated Runs on replaceable batteries. No truck wiring, no electrician, no downtime for installation.
  • Passenger-side placement Mounts on the front passenger-side corner — the angle where pedestrians and cross-traffic are most at risk.
  • Rugged mount Purpose-built bracket secures the unit to the truck body. Handles vibration, weather, and daily fleet use.
  • Safety-first design A visual warning system, not a tracking or monitoring device. Your drivers stay private — everyone around the truck stays safer.
