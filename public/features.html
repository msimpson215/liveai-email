Features

Four pillars. One simple system. Everything your crew needs on the job site.

  • Visible Warning System High-visibility flashing red beacon on the safety base — always on when the pole is mounted. Pedestrians, crew, and drivers get an unmistakable visual alert.
  • Auto Power & Connect Drop the pole into the hood mount and power connects automatically. Camera and hotspot link up without switches, wiring, or a checklist.
  • Driver Alert System Optional in-cab buzzer sounds if the pole is up and the vehicle is shifted into gear — helping prevent costly mistakes before they happen.
  • Live 360° Streaming Watch the full job site from your office on any device. 360° camera gives complete situational awareness — improve safety, ensure quality, increase accountability.
  • 360° Camera Insta360 X3 class camera — 5.7K 360° video, WiFi / app control, battery powered.
  • Telescoping Pole Extends 8–15 ft. Lightweight, durable, collapses for storage in the truck.
  • Hood Mount Base Heavy-duty red base with reflective stripes. Quick install, locking pin secure. Fits most trucks.
  • Hotspot Phone Pre-configured phone in a secure in-cab mount — auto-connects for live streaming anywhere you have cell service.
