Axon AI — what it is

Axon AI is a conversational AI brain that can run on any mainstream AI model (or a combination of them) with smart routing. It makes websites, emails, cards, pamphlets, résumés, instructions — almost any material — talkable.

Each deployment is customized to one product or company, with guardrails so it only talks about that subject. It can act as a salesperson, customer service, an informative guide, or simply a team member.

2. Pre-De-Icer product family

Same Axon blue presentation style, separate product line. Invented by Marty — preventive, not just reactive.

  1. Pre-De-Icer Live

    Eco-friendly deicing agent you put on before the storm so frost, snow, and ice don’t bond. Works down to –82°F. Original idea: windshields. Now expanded to lots more surfaces and markets.

    Pre-De-Icer page →

  2. Traction De-Icer (granulated)

    Granulated formula with calcium / magnesium for ice control plus traction underfoot and under tires.

  3. Commercial / Industrial (thicker)

    Heavier, thicker product for roads, bridges, parking areas, and commercial vehicles like trucks — danger-zone and fleet use.

  4. Pre-EmberGuard

    Subcategory of the Pre-De-Icer chemistry idea: temporary fire-retardant gel for roofs, walls, eaves, fences. Spray on with a power sprayer or bug sprayer before ember threat. To remove: spray with water.
