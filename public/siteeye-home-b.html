How it works

No wiring. No IT. No overnight.

Truck-mounted 360° pole

Twist-lock suction base grips the hood — lock one way, release the other, zero paint damage. Telescopes 8–15 ft for a full view over the whole site.

Indoor jobsite stand

Remodel or storefront? Set it on a weighted tripod with lock-down extension poles — like a DeWalt work light, but it's watching the room.

Always-on 5G

Built-in hotspot on AT&T, T-Mobile, or Verizon. Anywhere there's signal, the feed is live — no site WiFi needed.

Ring-style dashboard

All your sites in one place on your monitors or phone. Click a job, watch the crew. Feeds auto-expire in 24 hours; save a clip when you need proof.
