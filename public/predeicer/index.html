How It Works

Three steps. No scraping.

Pre-De-Icer is preventive, not reactive. You treat the surface ahead of the weather instead of fighting ice after it forms.

Spray before the storm

Apply an even coat to a clean, dry windshield the night before snow or frost.

The film holds its ground

Its honey-like viscosity clings to the glass and resists wash-off, staying active through precipitation.

Wipers on, drive away

Ice and snow rest on top instead of bonding. One pass of the wipers and you have clear visibility from the first minute.

Why It's Different

Not a salt. Not a brine. Not an alcohol spray.

Pre-De-Icer is a completely different approach to winter — built to prevent the bond rather than melt it after the fact.

❄️

Extreme cold performance

Viscosity & longevity
