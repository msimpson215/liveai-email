What is SiteEye360°™?

SiteEye360°™ is a vehicle- or jobsite-mounted monitoring system built for real-world fleet use. A telescoping pole with a 360° camera, flashing safety beacon, and auto-connecting power base goes to work in seconds — giving your office a live view of the entire job site while a visible red warning keeps everyone around the truck alert.

No holes. No lost resale value.

We know fleet owners hate drilling into hoods — it hurts truck value and makes people hesitate. SiteEye360°™ is designed around non-permanent mount options: bumper clamps, suction-cup twist-lock bases, and standalone tripod setups. Hood pin mounts remain available for fleets that want a fixed install, but they're never the only choice.

Automatic. Simple. Reliable.

  • No switches to flip
  • Power connects automatically when the pole seats in the mount
  • Red beacon alerts everyone on site
  • Live 360° view streams to your office
  • Works with any hotspot

Built for safety — not surveillance

SiteEye360°™ is about keeping crews and the public safe, ensuring quality work, and increasing accountability on the job. It's a visual warning and monitoring system your team actually uses — because there's nothing to forget.
