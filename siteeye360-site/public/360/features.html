Features

Four pillars. One simple system. Mount it your way — no hood drilling required.

  • Visible Warning System High-visibility flashing red beacon on the safety base — always on when the pole is mounted. Pedestrians, crew, and drivers get an unmistakable visual alert.
  • Auto Power & Connect Drop the pole into the mount and power connects automatically. Camera and hotspot link up without switches, wiring, or a checklist.
  • Driver Alert System Optional in-cab buzzer sounds if the pole is up and the vehicle is shifted into gear — helping prevent costly mistakes before they happen.
  • Live 360° Streaming Watch the full job site from your office on any device. 360° camera gives complete situational awareness.
  • 360° Camera Insta360 X3 class camera — 5.7K 360° video, WiFi / app control, battery powered.
  • Telescoping Pole Extends 8–15 ft. Lightweight, durable, collapses for storage in the truck.
  • Hotspot Phone Pre-configured phone in a secure in-cab mount — auto-connects for live streaming anywhere you have cell service.

Mount options — pick what fits your fleet

Not every truck should get holes in the hood. Choose a mount that matches how you work:

Most popular

Bumper clamp mount

Heavy-duty clamp grips the front bumper or grille area. No drilling, no paint damage. Remove in seconds when you're done for the day.

No drill

Suction cup twist-lock

Industrial suction base with a twist-to-tighten lever. Turn one way to lock, the other to release. Works on hood, roof, or any smooth panel.

Tripod / jobsite stand

Skip the truck entirely. Set the pole on a weighted tripod beside the work zone — ideal for paving crews, inspections, or stationary monitoring.

Hood pin mount (optional)

Traditional locking-pin base for fleets that want a permanent dedicated truck. Quick install, but requires mounting holes — optional, not required.
