How It Works

Installs in under 30 seconds. No tools. No training manual. Works with bumper, suction, tripod, or hood mounts.

  1. Arrive at job site Park and set the brake. Attach your chosen mount — bumper clamp, suction cup, tripod, or hood base — in seconds. No drilling required for most setups.
  2. Insert pole Drop the telescoping pole into the mount and secure the locking pin (or twist-lock on suction bases). Power, beacon, and camera activate automatically.
  3. Auto connect The in-cab hotspot phone links up on its own. Live 360° stream is ready — no switches to flip.
  4. Watch live View the full 360° job site from your office on any device. Improve safety, ensure quality, watch from anywhere.
  5. Collapse & go Remove the pole, collapse it, and stow in the truck. Release suction or bumper clamps — open circuit shuts everything off until next job.

Mount in action

Suction twist-lock

Place on hood or panel → twist lever clockwise to vacuum-lock → reverse to release. No marks, no holes.

Bumper clamp

Clip onto bumper lip or grille bar → tighten hand knob → done. Protects truck resale value.

Tripod stand

Set beside the job — no truck attachment at all. Perfect when the truck moves but the camera shouldn't.
