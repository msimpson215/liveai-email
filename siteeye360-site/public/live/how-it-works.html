How It Works

Under five minutes from tailgate to live view.

1ArrivePark at jobsite
2DeployOpen tripod or attach clamp mount
3ConnectPower on — cellular links automatically
4ViewOpen app on phone or browser at office
5Pack upCollapse mast, load truck, done

Temporary by design

When the job ends, the unit comes off the truck and out of the picture. No permanent install. No holes. No "always watching" narrative for your crews — just visibility when you need it.
