Overview

Temporary remote visibility for owners and managers who can't be on every jobsite — but need to know what's going on today.

Built for the job — not the fleet

SiteEye LIVE™ is designed for short-term deployment. Show up, set the unit on a tripod or clamp mount, get a live 360° view on your phone, and pack up when the crew leaves. This isn't always-on employee tracking. It's peace of mind for the job you're running right now.

Perfect for

  • Asphalt & sealcoating crews
  • Landscaping & tree service
  • Roofing and concrete pours
  • Municipal and utility work
  • Events, festivals, and car shows
