Features

Commercial-grade hardware in a package you can deploy yourself — no integrator, no IT department.

  • 360° 4K camera — panoramic day & night, live stream to app
  • 12–15 ft telescoping mast — carbon fiber, collapses to ~3 ft
  • Lockable weatherproof enclosure — protects hotspot & battery
  • 4G / 5G cellular hotspot — works without jobsite Wi-Fi
  • Flashing beacon + motion alarm (Pro) — deters theft, alerts if moved
  • 8–12 hour battery — full shift on one charge
