The Problem: Winter Ice is Dangerous and Inconvenient

Winter ice poses significant challenges across various aspects of daily life, affecting roads, sidewalks, vehicles, and infrastructure. Traditional reactive measures—such as scraping, salting, and chipping away ice—are time-consuming, often ineffective in extreme cold, and can lead to environmental and infrastructural damage.​

Limitations of Traditional De-Icing Methods

One of the primary methods for combating ice is the application of road salt (sodium chloride). While effective at temperatures around 30°F (-1°C), its performance diminishes rapidly as temperatures drop. Below 15°F (-9°C), salt's ability to melt ice is significantly reduced, making it a less reliable solution during extreme cold spells. In such conditions, road crews often face challenges in maintaining safe and clear surfaces, as traditional salt treatments become ineffective. ​

The Human and Economic Costs

The consequences of ineffective ice management are severe. According to the Federal Highway Administration, over 1,300 people are killed and more than 116,800 are injured annually in vehicle crashes on snowy, slushy, or icy pavement. These statistics highlight the critical need for effective ice prevention methods to enhance safety during winter months. ​

Beyond vehicular accidents, winter conditions contribute to numerous pedestrian injuries. Snow shoveling, for instance, is responsible for thousands of injuries and as many as 100 deaths each year, often due to heart attacks induced by overexertion. ​National Safety Council

The Solution: Pre-De-Icer

Pre-De-Icer offers a proactive approach to combating winter ice, preventing its formation on various surfaces before it becomes a hazard. By applying Pre-De-Icer ahead of winter weather events, it creates a clear, non-toxic barrier that inhibits snow and freezing rain from adhering to surfaces such as bridges, overpasses, parking lots, and private roads.​

Key Benefits of Pre-De-Icer:

Proactive Protection: Applying Pre-De-Icer before a storm prevents ice from forming, reducing the need for reactive measures.​

Effective in Extreme Cold: Unlike traditional road salt, which loses effectiveness below 15°F (-9°C), Pre-De-Icer remains active at much lower temperatures, ensuring reliable performance during severe winter conditions.​

By integrating Pre-De-Icer into winter maintenance routines, municipalities, private companies, and property owners can enhance safety, protect infrastructure, and achieve more efficient and environmentally responsible ice management. Addressing the limitations of traditional methods and emphasizing the serious risks associated with winter ice underscores the importance of adopting innovative solutions like Pre-De-Icer.
