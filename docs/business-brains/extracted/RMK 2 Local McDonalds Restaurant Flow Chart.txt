At a Local McDonalds Restaurant (L.M.R.) a cashier references Table Tents on the Register and asks “Would you like to donate a Happy Meal to a child at St. Louis Childrens Hospital?”

	



Customer Donates $2.99 Happy Meal by paying for it at L.M.R. and receives a sticker saying “I Made a child at St. Louis Childrens Hospital Happy Today”. The purchase is recorded by the L.M.R and also electronically transmitted to “Ronald McDonald Kitchen (R.M.K.)”.  Then RMK invoices LMR for $2.49 giving the LMR 

Fifty cents for participating in the program. All transactions are recorded and documented.
