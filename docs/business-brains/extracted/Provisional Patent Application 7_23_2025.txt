Title of Invention:
VoxTalk: Voice-Activated, AI-Driven Website Assistant with Auto-Trigger Engagement
Background of the Invention:
As artificial intelligence and voice recognition technology continue to evolve, most
websites still rely on outdated user interfaces such as contact forms, generic chatbots,
or static content. These systems are impersonal, reactive, and often underutilized by site
visitors.
There exists a need for a simple, website-integrated, custom-branded, voice-to-voice AI
assistant that helps website owners engage visitors immediately — starting the
conversation as soon as they arrive on the domain. The system should work for legacy,
static, or dynamic websites, and allow businesses to offer customized, voice-guided
interaction based solely on their products, services, and internal knowledge.
This invention is designed to serve website owners and their customers, creating a better
user experience while protecting brand identity and keeping AI interaction focused on
the intended topic.
Summary of the Invention:
This invention provides a method and system for embedding a voice-first, AI-powered
assistant into any website. The assistant auto-triggers upon domain load, optionally

beginning with a text-based greeting such as, “Welcome to [Company Name], click here
to begin a voice conversation.” Users can then choose to engage using spoken
conversation or text, depending on their environment.
The AI system integrates with backend large language models (LLMs) such as OpenAI’s
GPT-4, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity, and it can be trained using the client’s proprietary
business information, enabling custom, branded responses. It avoids off-topic
discussion and can redirect irrelevant questions back to the business's focus area (e.g.,
“Sorry, I’m only trained to assist with Corvette parts”).
Detailed Description:
Core Features:
● Auto-launch on website load with text-first greeting
● Optional voice-to-voice interaction using microphone and speech synthesis
● Voice and text toggle for accessibility in quiet or noisy settings
● Custom-trained AI models that use the business’s own documents, FAQs, or
product catalogs
● Controlled response scope to stay on-brand and avoid generic open-ended AI
answers
● Integration with multiple backend models: GPT-4, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity
● Multi-API support for voice input/output and transcription, including:
○ OpenAI (ChatGPT)
○ Google Cloud Speech-to-Text
○ ElevenLabs for voice synthesis
○ WebSockets
○ Node.js or Python (Flask/Express) for backend

○ HTML, CSS, JavaScript for frontend widget
Technical Workflow:
1. User visits website → system auto-triggers with on-screen greeting
2. Option to enable microphone and begin voice conversation
3. Speech captured and transcribed using Google Cloud or Whisper
4. Transcription sent to AI backend (GPT, Gemini, etc.)
5. Response generated based on business-trained data only
6. Output played as synthesized voice using ElevenLabs
7. User may continue speaking or switch to typing
Use Cases:
● Auto industry (e.g., classic Corvette parts)
● Law firms and medical offices
● Local service businesses
● Educational or nonprofit sites
● eCommerce and online retail
● Municipal and tourism websites
