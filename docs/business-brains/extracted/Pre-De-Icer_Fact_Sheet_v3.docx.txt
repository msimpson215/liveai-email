Pre-De-Icer: Patent-Protected, Smarter Ice Prevention
Key Benefits:
● Designed for bridges and overpasses — the most dangerous freeze-prone locations.
● Thick, honey-like viscosity clings to surfaces and resists rain wash-off.
● Extreme cold performance — proven effective down to –51 °F (new formula to –82 °F).
● Eco-safe and salt-free — protects vehicles, concrete, and waterways.
● Longer-lasting protection reduces truck trips, fuel, and labor costs.
Never Scrape Again:
Windshields are one of the biggest opportunities for Pre-De-Icer. Apply before the storm
and eliminate scraping entirely. Drivers simply turn on the wipers and go — with clear
visibility from the first minute. Safe for paint, wipers, and trim, making it a natural fit for
companies like WeatherTech.
