Setting up a bottling plant involves several key pieces of equipment. Here are some fundamental components:

Filling Machines: For filling bottles with the product.

Capping Machines: For sealing bottles with caps.

Labeling Machines: For applying labels to the bottles.

Bottle Rinsers: For cleaning bottles before filling.

Conveyor Systems: For moving bottles through the production line.

Mixing Tanks: For mixing the product ingredients.

Pumps: For transferring liquids between tanks and filling machines.

Storage Tanks: For holding raw materials and finished products.

Shrink Wrapping Machines: For packaging bottles in groups.

Quality Control Systems: For ensuring consistency and quality.

As for the cost, a bottling plant with all these components can range significantly. For a medium-sized operation, you might be looking at anywhere from $100,000 to $500,000, depending on the level of automation and capacity.
