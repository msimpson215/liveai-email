Corporate Intro Letter

Dear McDonald’s Owner/Operator,

As part of our September initiative for Pediatric Cancer Awareness Month, we’re proud to introduce the Ronald McDonald Kitchen donation program—a simple and heartfelt way for customers to give back to children in local hospitals and Ronald McDonald Houses.

Enclosed is a complete promotional package you can implement with minimal effort. This includes:

– Employee training language – Table tent messaging for counters and dining areas – A branding overview for internal use

This initiative gives guests the opportunity to donate a cone or Happy Meal at checkout, offering them a moment of joy and purpose, and delivering real meals to children undergoing treatment nearby. Franchisees will retain a small portion of each donation to support participation, with the remainder going directly to serve these families.

We invite you to join us in making this program a success across the region.

Warm regards, [McDonald’s Regional/Corporate Name Here]
