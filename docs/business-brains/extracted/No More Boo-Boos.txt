No More Boo-Boos The dissolvable bandage that disappears without a tear.

Overview No More Boo-Boos is a breakthrough in wound care — a dissolvable bandage designed especially for children, but with broad medical potential. Made from natural starches and enriched with amino acids and peptides, it gently dissolves under warm water without tearing skin or disrupting healing. No pain. No drama. No peeling.

This isn’t just about comfort — it’s about kindness. Whether it’s a frightened child or a fragile elder, removing a bandage shouldn’t cause more trauma than the wound itself.

Why It Matters Walk down any drugstore aisle — traditional bandages haven’t changed in decades. Kids still cry when they’re peeled off. No More Boo-Boos does the opposite: it disappears painlessly. And in its place? Healing. Comfort. Even a little surprise — a cute animal character revealed as the bandage dissolves.
