What Pre-De-Icer Does
● Prevents ice from bonding before it forms—rather than melting ice after it’s already
dangerous
● Stays in place during precipitation—its viscosity resists wash-off on vertical and
angled surfaces
● Works in extreme cold—effective well below temperatures where salt, brine, and
sprays fail
● Reduces labor and risk—one preventive application replaces multiple reactive passes
● Protects vehicles and infrastructure—non-corrosive, paint-safe, and environmentally
responsible
Why It’s Different
Most winter products are reactive and short-lived. You wait for ice to form—then scrape, spray,
or reapply.

-- 1 of 3 --

Pre-De-Icer is preventive and long-lasting.
Its key differentiator is viscosity. Rather than a thin liquid that washes away, Pre-De-Icer forms
a gel-like coating that disrupts ice crystal formation at the surface. Ice does not adhere—and if
ice or snow is present, it releases instead of bonding.
This same viscosity allows the product to remain effective during changing conditions, including
light rain before snow.
The Result
Ice rests on top of the surface instead of bonding to it.
When ready to use, one swipe clears it—no scraping required.
Where It Applies
Windshields — the immediate opportunity
Think about a winter convenience store: scrapers, brushes, de-icers, washer fluid. Every
product is reactive. There is nothing designed to go on a windshield before a storm to prevent
ice in the first place.
Pre-De-Icer changes that.
Apply it before the event. When it’s time to drive, turn on the wipers—snow and ice slide off.
Immediate visibility. No scraping. No frozen wipers. No exposure.
