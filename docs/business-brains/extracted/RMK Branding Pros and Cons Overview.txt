Branding Pros and Cons Overview

Positive Branding Power: – Shows McDonald’s as family-centered and community-driven – Reinforces loyalty through local impact – Inspires positive press and social engagement – Boosts employee morale and pride – Offsets negative perceptions around price hikes with real heart

Potential Risks (and How We Address Them): – Donation Fatigue: Keep the ask gentle, once per visit – Confusion: Clear signage and cashier training – Cost Concerns: Participating franchisees receive a small portion of each donation – Media scrutiny: Transparent messaging avoids overpromising
