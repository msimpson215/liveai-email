D) “Other pre-icers” claim (why PDI is
different)
● Commodity “de-icers” for windshields are typically alcohol-heavy, reactive sprays
(e.g., methanol/ethanol based; see Prestone SDS). They melt after the bond forms and
can be harsh on materials. PDI is a preventive coating with viscosity/adhesion that
stops bonding and coats blades so they don’t freeze mid-drive. Mighty Auto Parts+1
● Brines (liquid NaCl) are cheap but also salt-temperature-limited (15–20°F practical).
