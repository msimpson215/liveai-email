Executive Summary

Pre-De-Icer is a breakthrough anti-icing solution designed to prevent the formation of ice/snow on windshields, roadways, bridges, airplane wings, power lines, and other surfaces. Unlike traditional de-icers, which reactively melt ice after it forms, Pre-De-Icer is applied before a storm, forming a long-lasting, eco-friendly barrier that stops ice from bonding in the first place.

The product is all natural, safe for vehicle paint and door locks, and doubles as a salt remover—cleaning the underside of vehicles while preventing rust It also coats the wiper blades keeping them from freezin while you drive. Plus, actually cleans the windshield from road grim salt residue. It has a freezing point as low as -82°F, outperforming traditional salt and brine solutions(lowest -20F), and works effectively in even harsher environments.

Our goal is to introduce Pre-De-Icer to the automotive market first, then residential application(driveways and walkways), and then into municipal, aeronautical, nautical and agricultural sectors. The product is patented,and we are currently seeking investment to fund phase one of three phases for the grassroots campaign(phase one) then followed by near future manufacturing, packaging, and distribution.

Problem & Market Opportunity

Every winter, millions of people battle ice—scraping windshields, salting driveways, and struggling to get their cars started in freezing temps. Municipalities spend billions on road salt, which corrodes infrastructure, damages vehicles, and pollutes the environment. Airlines face delays and risks from wing icing. Orchards lose crops to sudden frost events. The problem is massive and widespread.

Pre-De-Icer solves these challenges by shifting the approach from reactive to preventive. With one application before a storm, ice simply can’t stick. Once we reach full marketing potential, we estimate a total worldwide addressable market of over $2 billion annually across automotive, residential, municipal, aviation, and agricultural sectors.



Product Description

Pre-De-Icer is a viscous, non-toxic spray formulated to prevent ice from bonding to surfaces. It clings to glass, metal, and plastic, offering protection for hours or days depending on conditions. It doesn’t damage paint or clear coats, and it's safe for the environment.

For consumers, it’s packaged in trigger bottles or battery-powered sprayers and refillable gallon jugs. For cities and DOTs, it can be applied using existing brine sprayers. In agriculture, it’s being adapted to protect fruit trees from frost using an exothermic reaction that gently warms buds overnight.

Key features:

Works before the storm—no scraping needed

Freezing point: -82°F

Formulation all from natural ingredents

Safe on all vehicle finishes

Eco-friendly and non-corrosive

Cleans road salt off undercarriages
