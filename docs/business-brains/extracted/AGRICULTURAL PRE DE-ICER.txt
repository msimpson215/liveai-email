AGRICULTURAL  PRE DE-ICER

2024

OVERVIEW & PURPOSE

Agricultural Pre De-Icer is a Eco friendly Organic viscous solution that helps prevent Crop freeze.  Activating the solution creates an organic exothermic reaction when applied directly on the crop, thereby preventing it from freezing.

APPLICATIONS and BENEFITS

Only credible product available to help prevent crop freeze

Market to Fruit Orchards and Farmers to help prevent billions of dollars lost annually to crop freeze.

Market to Vineyards to prevent cold injury or freeze to grapes during green tissue after bud break. 

Develop a direct to consumer market for Home Garden use. 

Due to Climate Change, weather fluctuations are predicted to continue, warrentening the need for a product like  Agricultural Anti Ice.
