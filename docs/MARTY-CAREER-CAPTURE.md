## Note on the Business Journal photo

Scanning the printed page and posting it is the paper's copyright, not ours, and
it is exposure we do not need for no gain.

What a serious CV does instead is **cite** it — publication, date, headline, and
a link, as an entry on the Media page that already exists. Same credibility,
nothing to defend.

If the clipping itself is wanted as an image, that requires written permission
from the publication. Worth asking for; not worth assuming.
