## Naming note: SiteEye vs WorkSite I 360

**Job site** = construction industry standard for where work happens.

For **one-day, in-and-out** work (paving crew arrives, finishes, leaves):
- **Mobilized job** / **mobilization** — crews "mobilize" to a location temporarily
- **Field deployment** — equipment sent to a location for a short assignment
- **Temporary work site** — plain English
- **Active work zone** — safety/OSHA framing

**WorkSite I 360** — emphasizes temporary field work (Joe's paving crews, contractors).
**EventSite I 360** — fairs, festivals, one-day events.

Current demo URL still uses `src=siteeye` until you pick a final brand name.
