## The problem

Joe (and owners like him) runs the business in his head:

- Conversations on the job  
- Numbers in QuickBooks  
- SOPs in binders/PDFs  
- Follow-ups that never get written down  

ChatGPT doesn’t know his books. QuickBooks AI doesn’t know his field. Wearables remember talk but don’t run the business. Nobody wraps **ops + books + memory + voice** as **one assistant that belongs to him**.

## Business model — keep it Axon AI (don’t “sell the box”)

You’re right: **don’t sell a one-time product and walk away.**

You can’t support 100 custom installs, hardware returns, and orphaned accounts.

**Model:**

- **Axon AI** owns and runs the platform  
- Customer pays a **monthly subscription**  
- They get *their* named assistant (Joe’s Professional Assistant)  
- Axon hosts, updates, connects QB, support  
- Optional: white-label for other owners (Howard Tree, etc.) under Axon  

Same play as “Powered by Axon AI” — **Intel Inside**, not “we sold you a CD and disappeared.”
